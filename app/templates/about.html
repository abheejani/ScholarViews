The Story

ScholarViews started because we noticed a gap. Students were spending hundreds on LeetCode premium, generic courses, and career fairs — yet still bombing interviews because they had never practiced with someone who had actually sat in that room.

We built a platform where current engineers at places like Google, Meta, and Amazon could offer structured, high-quality mock interviews to students preparing for the same roles. Real prep, real feedback, real results.

Every session is 1-on-1. Every interviewer is vetted. Every piece of feedback is written and actionable. That's the ScholarViews standard.
