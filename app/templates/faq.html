Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ScholarViews, sessions, and pricing.

Getting Started
What is ScholarViews? +
ScholarViews is a peer-to-peer mock interview platform. We connect college students and recent grads with current interns and engineers at top tech companies for 1-on-1 interview prep sessions.
Who are the interviewers? +
Our interviewers are current students, interns, and full-time engineers at companies like Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Tesla. They have all recently gone through the exact hiring process you are preparing for. All interviewers are vetted before joining.
Do I need an account to browse? +
You can browse the homepage, pricing, about, and resources pages without an account. Scheduling sessions and resume reviews require a free account.
Sessions & Scheduling
What does a session look like? +
Each session is 1 hour. You will work through technical or behavioral questions (your choice) with your interviewer, just like a real interview. At the end, you receive structured written feedback with specific areas to improve and resources to use.
How do I book a session? +
Create an account, purchase a session package, then go to the Schedule page. You will see available time slots from all our interviewers. Click "Book" on a slot that works for you — it will be confirmed instantly and deducted from your credits.
Can I pick which interviewer I want? +
Yes. The schedule page shows availability grouped by interviewer so you can choose based on their company, specialization, and time slot that works for you.
Can I cancel or reschedule a session? +
Cancellation and rescheduling features are coming soon. For now, email us at scholarviewsinc@gmail.com and we will sort it out for you.
Pricing & Credits
How does pricing work? +
We sell session packages — 1 session ($20), 10 sessions ($180, save $20), or 20 sessions ($300, save $100). Each session = 1 credit = 1 hour with an interviewer. You pay once upfront and use credits whenever you are ready.
Do session credits expire? +
No. Your credits never expire. Buy when you are ready, use them on your own timeline. No pressure, no deadlines.
How do I pay? +
Secure payment processing is coming soon. Right now, email us at scholarviewsinc@gmail.com and we will set up your account manually. We accept Venmo, Zelle, and PayPal.
Resume Review
Is resume review free? +
Yes. Resume review is free and unlimited for all registered users — no session credits required. Just upload your resume and expect feedback within 48 hours.
What format should I upload my resume in? +
We accept PDF, DOC, and DOCX formats. Please name your file in the format: FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf so we can keep track of submissions.
How long does resume feedback take? +
We aim to get feedback back to you within 48 hours of submission. During busy periods it may take up to 72 hours. You will receive feedback directly by email.
Grad Studies
What is Grad Studies? +
Grad Studies will offer research paper reviews, statement of purpose editing, and 1-on-1 grad school mentorship from current PhD and Masters students. This feature is coming soon — email us to be notified when it launches.

Still have a question?

Email us at scholarviewsinc@gmail.com and we will get back to you within 24 hours.
